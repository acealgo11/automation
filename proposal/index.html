What Ace Automation does

Ace Automation trades currencies for you to a fixed set of rules. It buys and sells 20 of the main currency pairs, such as the pound against the dollar, through all market hours from Monday to Friday. Once it is connected there are no charts to analyse and no trades for you to place.

The account is yours

Your money sits in your own account at our regulated broker partner, in your name. Ace Automation can place trades on it but cannot withdraw from it.

Every trade is visible

Each trade appears in MetaTrader, the platform your account runs on, as it is placed. We also send you a summary each day.

You can stop at any time

There is no minimum term and no fee to leave. Tell us first, so that open trades can be closed in an orderly way.

R2 places trades twice the size of R1, so its gains and its drawdowns have both been roughly double. Closed and open trades

The headline gain counts closed trades only, which is how Myfxbook draws its growth chart. The figure that includes open trades also counts positions still open at a loss, so it reads lower while the account is in drawdown.

Floating drawdown

This measures how far open trades fell below the account's previous high before they were closed. Both accounts have since reached new highs. That is a matter of record and is not a guarantee.

Your own results

Both track-record accounts are held in GBP at Vantage. New clients trade through our partner broker and will start at a different time with a different balance, so their results will differ. Your capital is at risk.

Risk management

Ace Automation keeps positions open through adverse moves instead of closing them at a loss straight away, so the account will spend periods in floating drawdown. You choose one of two risk levels, R1 or R2, and the limits below are applied automatically.

Drawdown pause

New entries stop when drawdown reaches 25% on R1 or 50% on R2. Open trades continue to be managed after a pause, so drawdown can in principle go beyond these levels. Neither account has reached its limit to date.

Position sizing

Trade size is calculated from the balance: 0.01 lots per £1,000 on R1 and 0.02 on R2. On a £5,000 account that is 0.05 lots on R1 and 0.10 lots on R2. Sizes rise with the account and may be reduced slightly during a drawdown to protect margin.

Margin floor

The margin level is kept above 200%, and the number of positions open at any one time scales with the size of the account.

Volatility response

A recovery routine reduces exposure quickly when market conditions become volatile.

Trading leveraged FX carries a high risk of loss, and you should only invest money you can afford to lose. Please read the full risk disclosure.

Ace Automation suits people who want their money to work in the background and who can give it time to compound.

It may suit you if you

  • want your money working through market hours without trading it yourself
  • prefer an automated approach with full sight of every trade

  • want a guaranteed return

Your questions answered

Choose a question to see the answer.

How do I know this is not a Ponzi scheme?

Your money never comes to us. It stays in your own account at our partner broker, in your name, and Ace Automation has permission to trade on it but not to withdraw from it. Every return comes from trades that you can see in MetaTrader. Nobody is paid from anybody else's deposits, and no new money is needed to pay anyone.

Is Ace Automation a fund, and do you pool clients' money?

No. There is no pool and no fund to buy into. Each client has a separate broker account in their own name, and Ace Automation trades each one individually.

How can I check that Ace Automation is genuine?

Our R1 and R2 accounts are public on Myfxbook, which shows the full trade-by-trade history and can be inspected by anyone. New clients' accounts are held at our partner broker, a regulated broker, and your money stays in your own account. We also share client results openly with our community on WhatsApp.

Can I lose money, and how deep has drawdown been?

Yes, you can lose money. Ace Automation keeps trades open through adverse moves, so the account spends periods in floating drawdown, which is the fall in value while trades are still open, measured from the account's previous high. The largest so far is 23.05% on R1 and 46.47% on R2. Drawdown builds gradually and reduces as trades close in profit, and both accounts have since reached new highs, which is not guaranteed to happen again. Only invest capital that you can afford to lose and could leave in place through a deep drawdown.

Can I withdraw my money whenever I want?

Yes. Your funds stay in your own brokerage account, and Ace Automation never holds them or withdraws them. The only money that leaves the account for us is our profit share, which is deducted automatically on partner broker accounts. You withdraw directly through the broker, and bank transfers usually arrive within two to five working days. Please message us before a large withdrawal so that open trades can be managed properly.

What does it cost, and are there other fees?

Connecting and running Ace Automation is free. The only charge is 20% of the profit from closed trades. For clients at our partner broker it is deducted automatically at the end of each month, or when you stop using Ace Automation, and no fee is due for a month without profit. There are no subscription, set-up, data or exit fees, and no minimum term. Ace Automation also receives a volume rebate from the broker; this is paid by the broker and is not deducted from your account.

Can I change my lot size?

Not directly. Lot size is set automatically from your balance and your risk level, and it grows as the balance grows. Ace Automation also monitors margin and may reduce sizes slightly during a drawdown to protect the account. If you would like more or less risk, speak to us about moving between R1 and R2. Please do not change trades yourself, as this interferes with how Ace Automation manages risk.
