Closed and open trades

The headline gain counts closed trades only, which is how Myfxbook draws its growth chart. The figure that includes open trades also counts positions still open at a loss, so it reads lower while the account is in drawdown.

Floating drawdown

This measures how far open trades fell below the account's previous high before they were closed. Both accounts have since reached new highs. That is a matter of record and is not a guarantee.

Your own results

Both track-record accounts are held in GBP at Vantage. New clients trade through our partner broker and will start at a different time with a different balance, so their results will differ. Your capital is at risk.

How the strategy works

Ace Automation trades to a fixed set of rules and makes no discretionary decisions. It runs on our servers, so there is nothing for you to install or watch over.

Selection

The software measures momentum across the eight major currencies continuously and trades the differences between them on 20 currency pairs. A trade is opened when its rules are met, and at no other time.

Position management

Positions are built in stages rather than in a single entry. Each stage is sized to the account balance and closed at set profit targets, which keeps exposure in proportion as the account grows or falls.

Infrastructure

The engine runs on dedicated cloud servers through all market hours, independently of your own computer. The connection to your account is encrypted, and each trade appears in your MT4 or MT5 terminal as it is placed.

Drawdown pause

New entries stop when drawdown reaches 25% on R1 or 50% on R2. Open trades continue to be managed after a pause, so drawdown can in principle go beyond these levels. Neither account has reached its limit to date.

Margin floor

The margin level is kept above 200%, and the number of positions open at any one time scales with the size of the account.

Volatility response

A recovery routine reduces exposure quickly when market conditions become volatile.

No manual trading

Please do not place or close trades by hand on a connected account, as this interferes with how Ace Automation manages risk. If you want to withdraw or stop, tell us first so that open trades can be closed in an orderly way.

Questions

Getting started

How do I get started, and how long does it take?

Opening and funding a broker account takes around 45 minutes. You can begin with our client sign-up link and then message the team so that we can connect Ace Automation. Once we have your details, Ace Automation is usually trading within 12 to 24 hours. After that there are no charts to analyse and no trades for you to place or manage.

How much do I need to start?

The minimum balance is $10,000. Trade sizes are set from your balance, so profits and losses move in proportion to the account. You can add funds later if you wish.

Which broker do I need, and can I use one I already have?

New clients connect through our partner broker on MetaTrader 4 or MetaTrader 5, so you will need a live account with them in your own name. There is nothing to install, and our team collects the login details directly with you.

Do I need to keep my computer on?

No. Ace Automation runs on cloud servers rather than on your own machine, so it trades through market hours whether your computer is on or off.

Fees and withdrawals

What does it cost, and are there other fees?

Connecting and running Ace Automation is free. The only charge is 20% of the profit from closed trades. For clients at our partner broker it is deducted automatically at the end of each month, or when you stop using Ace Automation, and no fee is due for a month without profit. There are no subscription, set-up, data or exit fees, and no minimum term. Ace Automation also receives a volume rebate from the broker; this is paid by the broker and is not deducted from your account.

Can I withdraw my money whenever I want?

Yes. Your funds stay in your own brokerage account, and Ace Automation never holds them or withdraws them. The only money that leaves the account for us is our profit share, which is deducted automatically on partner broker accounts. You withdraw directly through the broker, and bank transfers usually arrive within two to five working days. Please message us before a large withdrawal so that open trades can be managed properly.

What happens if I want to stop?

You can stop at any time. Please message us on WhatsApp first, so that we can wind the account down and close open trades in an orderly way. If an account is simply disconnected, any open trades are closed automatically at market, which during a drawdown can turn a temporary loss into a realised one. Any fee already due remains payable.

I am already a client. What applies to me?

If you are already connected through another broker, nothing changes for you. Clients who joined before our current broker partnership are invoiced for their profit share. Existing members on a fixed monthly plan keep that plan and can manage it on the member page. Tiers are assessed on the account's average balance across the billing month, and account size includes any trading credit bonuses.

Risk

Can I lose money, and how deep has drawdown been?

Yes, you can lose money. Ace Automation keeps trades open through adverse moves, so the account spends periods in floating drawdown, which is the fall in value while trades are still open, measured from the account's previous high. The largest so far is 23.05% on R1 and 46.47% on R2. Drawdown builds gradually and reduces as trades close in profit, and both accounts have since reached new highs, which is not guaranteed to happen again. Only invest capital that you can afford to lose and could leave in place through a deep drawdown.

What is the win rate?

On the two public Myfxbook accounts, 1,002 of 1,056 trades on R1 and 1,177 of 1,268 on R2 have closed in profit, which is around 94%. A high win rate does not mean low risk. Floating drawdown is a normal part of how Ace Automation trades, returns vary from month to month, and you can lose money.

What protects my account?

Ace Automation applies 15 risk-management rules automatically. The main ones are these: new entries pause at 25% drawdown on R1 and 50% on R2, the number of open positions scales with account size, the margin level is kept above 200%, lot size is set from the balance, and a recovery routine reduces exposure quickly in volatile conditions. Open trades are still managed after a pause, so drawdown can in principle go beyond those levels, although neither account has reached them so far.

Can I change my lot size?

Not directly. Lot size is set automatically from your balance and your risk level, and it grows as the balance grows. Ace Automation also monitors margin and may reduce sizes slightly during a drawdown to protect the account. If you would like more or less risk, speak to us about moving between R1 and R2. Please do not change trades yourself, as this interferes with how Ace Automation manages risk.
