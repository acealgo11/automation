02 / The basics

What is algorithmic trading?

In plain English: it's software that follows a fixed set of trading rules automatically. It reads market data, decides whether the rules say to act, and places the order on your account. No one is sitting there clicking buttons.

Input

Market data

Live prices across the major currency pairs, streamed in real time.

Logic

Fixed rules

The system checks every price movement against its rules. No opinions, no hunches.

Output

Order executed

When the rules are met, the trade is placed and managed on your broker account.

A.

It follows rules, not feelings

A losing streak doesn't make it nervous, and a winning streak doesn't make it greedy. It does exactly what it was built to do.

B.

It watches everything at once

A person can track a chart or two. Software can monitor every pair it trades, every second the market is open.

C.

It never clocks off

Forex trades around the clock from Sunday night to Friday night. The system covers the Sydney, Tokyo, London and New York sessions without a break.

What it isn't
  • Not a get-rich-quick scheme.It's a tool for disciplined, long-term execution. Losing periods happen.
  • Not a guarantee.Trading carries real risk and past performance never guarantees future results.
  • Not a fund.We never hold your money. It stays in your own broker account, in your name.

09 / FAQ

Questions people always ask.

Is this legit, or is it a scam?+
Fair question in this industry. Your money never comes to us. It stays in your own regulated broker account, in your name, and you can withdraw it at any time. Our results come from live accounts with public, independently verified trade histories that you can check yourself.
Can I lose money?+
Yes. Forex trading carries real risk, losing periods are a normal part of how any system trades, and past performance does not guarantee future results. Only use money you can afford to lose.
Do I need any trading experience?+
No. The software handles analysis, entries, exits and risk management. You don't need to read charts or place trades. We'll walk you through setup on the call.
Who holds my money?+
You do. Funds stay in your own account with the broker. The software can place trades on that account but has no ability to withdraw or move money.
How much do I need to start?+
The minimum account balance is £X,XXX. Start with an amount you're comfortable with, because you can always add more later.
Do I need to keep my computer on?+
No. The system runs on cloud servers, so it keeps working whether your computer is on, off, or you're on holiday.
Can I withdraw whenever I want?+
Yes. Withdrawals are made directly through your broker and are entirely in your control.
What happens on the call?+
We'll ask a few questions about your goals and experience, explain exactly how the system works and what it costs, and answer anything you're unsure about. If it isn't the right fit, we'll say so.
